# Sunny fantasy enemy sprites

This folder replaces the animal enemy presentation in the Sunny expedition with
non-animal fantasy monsters. Combat data still comes from the validated enemy
mechanism catalog; these files provide authored silhouettes and animation only.

- Source: https://ansimuz.itch.io/sunnyland-enemies-extended-pack
- Downloaded archive: `Sunny land Add On Files.zip`

- License: CC0. The included `public-license.pdf` explicitly permits personal
  and commercial use, modification, and redistribution without attribution.
- Imported files:
  - `spring_mushroom.png`: small spring mushroom, 7 frames at 32x39.
  - `thorn_guardian_shoot.png`: piranha plant shooting, 12 frames at 61x45.
  - `thorn_guardian_hurt.png`: piranha plant hurt, 8 frames at 61x45.

The original license PDF is retained beside the imported files as
`public-license.pdf`.
