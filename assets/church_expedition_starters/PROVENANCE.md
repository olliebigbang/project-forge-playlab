# Church expedition starter weapons

These are explicitly labelled, previously generated AI weapons, not a response
to new player input and never a fallback after failed generation. A player may
choose one for an offline expedition or generate an entirely new object.

Sources: 2026-08-30 live Church Forge garden fork, red coffee pot and padlocked
chain; semantic model recorded as claude-sonnet-5, FAL GPT Image 1.5 + image2pixel,
church_v1 palette. Final same-image grip/structure recompile and independent
visual review: `.tools/church-ai-forge/review-1788091483-24608`.

Two additional previously generated, identity-validated firearm samples (M4A1
and Smith & Wesson Model 686) were imported from the existing local AI cache
through `tests/prepare_expedition_firearm_samples.gd`. This preparation made
new Church-palette copies and recompiled their existing mechanisms; it did not
generate images or overwrite their source caches. They are explicitly offered
as existing samples, not as newly generated answers to input.

Packages retain their exact PNGs, mechanism declarations, anchors and integrity
hashes. There are no API keys, prompts that execute code, or runtime API calls in
this catalogue. Source Church environment/character art provenance remains in
`assets/art_vertical_slice_v1`.
