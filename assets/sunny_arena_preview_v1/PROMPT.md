## First composition (retained as v1)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual 2D pixel-game arena BACKGROUND, landscape 16:9. The two input images are STYLE REFERENCES for the freely licensed SunnyLand art pack by Ansimuz, not a layout to copy literally. Create ONE finished background image, no montage or labels. Match their deliberate chunky pixel clusters, saturated turquoise sky, pale cyan block-shaped clouds, layered blue-green foliage, orange-brown earth, dark teal outlines, small warm timber cottage, and bright grass tufts. Important: convert the narrow side-platform scene into a SIDE-VIEW BELT-SCROLLER CLEARING with a BROAD HORIZONTAL WALKABLE GROUND PLANE with near/far depth. This is NOT a top-down farming map and NOT a jumping platform level. Preserve the side-on facade and upright trees. Compose sky/clouds and distant blue-green forest in the upper 45%; a small grass-roofed SunnyLand-like timber cottage far back at the left edge and palms/trees at the right edge; the horizon/back edge of playable ground about y=45%. The lower half, y=46% to 92%, is a newly drawn connected broad level clearing: mostly calm warm sandy ochre dirt, a few subtle sparse horizontal flattened pixel texture marks, irregular patches of low green grass confined to the boundaries. It should be visibly a walkable SURFACE, not a vertically stretched platform cliff face. NO perspective grid, NO long cracks or flagstones. The central 75% of the ground must stay completely open and very low contrast for characters and attack warning overlays; at least four characters' depth between back and front edges. A slim grassy lip and a 3% tall earth edge at the very bottom can frame it; no giant vertical dirt wall. Fresh light adventurous atmosphere, not gloomy, not painterly. Uniform coarse pixel grid, ideally appearance of a 640x360 master displayed exactly 2x, hard crisp edges, compact palette, no antialiasing, no gradients, no dithering noise. No characters, weapons, enemies, UI, text, arrows, watermarks, floating platforms or new gameplay objects. Fill the whole 16:9 image. This is the ground/environment art sample only.

## Final selected refinement (v2)

Use case: style-transfer. Image 1 is the environment edit target. Image 2 is the original SunnyLand pixel style reference. Make ONE targeted refinement: strongly simplify the pixel rendering of Image 1 to match the coarse deliberate clusters of Image 2. Preserve the SAME wide 16:9 side-view clearing layout, sky and clouds, small grass-roofed house at left, trees at sides, horizon, warm empty broad ground, and slim bottom edge. Do not add or remove scene objects. Keep the central walkable floor empty. The problem in Image 1 is excessive fine noisy foliage and too many near-identical colors compared with the source sprites. Redraw with LARGE readable grouped clusters, use only 3 or 4 discrete colors per material, flat cyan sky without gradients, broad simple cloud shapes, no speckling or single-pixel noise in bushes/trees/grass. The intended actual game art resolution is 320x180 logical pixels with integer 4x enlargement, like a genuine low-resolution 16-bit game screen. Make all visible pixels the same size throughout; leaves must be simple grouped shapes, not tiny detailed leaf texture. Ground is mostly flat warm ochre with just a few widely separated low-contrast horizontal pixel marks. Crisp nearest-neighbor edges only; NO smoothing, blur, dither, grain, realistic shading or painterly gradients. No UI/text/characters/weapons/enemies. This refinement changes pixel discipline/density, not the approved broad horizontal ground composition.
